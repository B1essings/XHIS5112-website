How to use this portal

Using the Service Portal

The portal is designed to make support simple and consistent. Follow these steps for the best experience.

  1. Submit a Ticket - go to the Submit a Ticket page and complete the short form. You will receive an acknowledgement email with your Incident ID.
  2. Check the Knowledge Base - for common problems like Wi-Fi or password issues, the knowledge base may solve it faster than waiting for a technician.
  3. Download forms - need a change to an IT system? Download and complete the Change Request form from the Knowledge Base page.
  4. Track performance - visit the Dashboard page to see live statistics such as tickets logged, open, resolved, and average resolution time.
  5. Meet the team - the Team page shows who supports you and how to reach them.
