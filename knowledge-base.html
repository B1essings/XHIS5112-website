FAQs & Troubleshooting

Frequently asked questions

Click a question to reveal the answer. Most common issues are resolved here without logging a ticket.

Why is my Wi-Fi not working?

Work through these steps in order:

1. Confirm Wi-Fi is switched on and Airplane mode is off.
2. Restart your laptop and the nearest Wi-Fi access point.
3. Forget the network and reconnect to "NovaTech-Corp".
4. Check whether colleagues nearby have the same problem. If the whole area is offline, the fault is likely a switch or access point, not your device.
5. Still offline after these steps? Log a ticket under category "Network" and mention your building and floor. If downtime exceeds 30 minutes, the ticket is escalated immediately.

How do I reset my password?

Open the NovaTech portal, select "Forgot password", enter your employee ID, and follow the link sent to your registered email. If the link does not arrive within 10 minutes, log a ticket under "Access & Accounts".

How do I set up VPN on my laptop?

Install the NovaTech VPN client from the software portal, sign in with your NovaTech credentials, and select the office region. Keep the client updated; older versions are the most common cause of failed connections.

My printer is not responding. What should I check?

Confirm the printer is powered on and connected to the network, check for paper jams, and restart the print spooler on your PC (services.msc, restart "Print Spooler"). If other users cannot print either, log a ticket under "Hardware".

What do the ticket priorities mean?

Low - minor inconvenience, no work blocked (e.g. single monitor flickering).
Medium - some work impact but you can continue (e.g. software crashing on save).
High - work is stopped or multiple users are affected (e.g. office Wi-Fi down). High-priority tickets are escalated immediately.

How we escalate

Escalation policy

Every ticket follows a documented three-level process with clear response targets.

1

Level 1 - Service Desk

First-line support responds within 2 hours. Handles password resets, known fixes, and initial diagnosis.

2

Level 2 - Specialists

Escalated when Level 1 cannot resolve the issue. Responds within 24 hours and handles deeper technical faults.

3

Level 3 - Experts

Escalated for complex infrastructure or vendor cases. Responds within 48 hours or more for complex cases.

Escalation triggers: a ticket is escalated when it remains unresolved after two follow-ups, or when network downtime exceeds 30 minutes. Because most tickets involve network infrastructure, technicians first check the path from your device to the LAN: cabling, switch ports, routers, and access points, before moving up a level.
