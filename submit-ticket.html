How it works

How to log a problem

Follow these simple steps. The whole process takes less than five minutes.

  1. Complete the form below - fill in your category, priority, and a short description of the issue.
  2. Receive your acknowledgement - an automatic email confirms your Incident ID and the expected response time.
  3. Wait for first contact - a Level 1 technician responds within 2 hours.
  4. Track to resolution - the ticket is escalated if needed and closed once you confirm the fix.
Expected response times: Level 1 within 2 hours, Level 2 within 24 hours, Level 3 within 48 hours (or longer for complex cases). Escalation happens automatically if an issue is unresolved after two follow-ups or network downtime exceeds 30 minutes.
